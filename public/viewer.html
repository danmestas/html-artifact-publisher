Artifact details

People with access

Named access belongs to a signed-in account. It is separate from capability links and from public snapshots.

Guest participants

Guest names and emails are self-identified, not verified. Emails are private to the guest and the artifact owner or instance owner. Named access is granted only after the guest signs in and claims this browser identity.

Public snapshot

A public, standalone copy of one version, with no sign-in required. Every file in this version becomes public, including images, scripts, and supporting files. Other versions, named access and private page data are not included.

Published snapshots

Link access

Capability links work on their own, without signing in. Anyone holding one has exactly the access the link carries. These are different from named access and from public snapshots.

Link previews show the artifact title and a topic icon publicly, even for private artifacts. Page content stays protected. Messaging apps may cache previews.

Your comment identity

You can comment without an account. Your name is shown with your comments as unverified. Email is optional and visible only to you and the artifact owner or instance owner.

Without a name, comments appear as Guest. This browser remembers your identity for up to 30 days. Clearing cookies loses that continuity. Your identity does not grant access without a valid artifact link.

To request named access, ask the owner for an invitation, sign in, then return in this browser and link your identity. The owner must approve access separately.

Comments are shared discussion. They do not ask an agent to act.
