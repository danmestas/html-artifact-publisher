# Charts and data visualization for artifacts

A chart in one of our artifacts is an HTML page rendered inside the viewer's sandboxed
iframe, under a strict Content-Security-Policy, on whatever screen the reader has. That
environment decides most of the technical choices before taste does:

- **No network.** The CSP has no remote hosts, so a `<script src="https://cdn...">` for
  Chart.js, D3, Plotly, Vega or Mermaid simply never loads. The default is **hand-written
  inline SVG** (or `<canvas>` for thousands of points) generated by a small inline script.
- **No eval.** `eval`, `new Function` and string `setTimeout` are blocked. Inline event
  attributes (`onclick=`, `onmouseover=`) are blocked. Attach every handler with
  `addEventListener`.
- **No downloads.** "Save as PNG" / "Export CSV" buttons do nothing in the sandbox. If data
  must leave the page, show it in a `<textarea>` the reader can select and copy.
- **One theme source.** The viewer has no toggle; the page follows the OS through
  `prefers-color-scheme`. Every chart color is a CSS custom property with a dark override.
- **Any width.** Pages are read from ~360 px phones to wide desktops.

Page-level layout, type and tokens belong to the **artifact-design** skill; getting the file
into a Fossil-versioned artifact belongs to **publish-html-page**. This skill is the chart part.

## Order of work

Colour is the last decision, not the first.

1. **Decide whether it is a chart at all.** One number that matters → a stat tile. Three
   numbers → a tile row. A dozen exact values the reader will look up → a table. A chart
   earns its space when the *shape* (trend, ranking, distribution, gap) is the message.
2. **Pick the form from the data's job** (table below).
3. **Assign colour by job** - identity, magnitude, polarity or status - using the palette here.
4. **Draw marks, axes and labels** to the specs below.
5. **Add hover** for any plotted chart; add a table view for anything non-trivial.
6. **Look at it** in both themes and at phone width before publishing. Render it locally
   (open the HTML in a browser, toggle the OS / devtools colour scheme, narrow the window).

## Choosing the form

| The reader needs to see... | Use | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| how big each item is, compared | horizontal bars (long labels) or columns (short labels, few items), sorted | pie with > 3 slices, 3-D anything |
| change over a continuous axis (time) | line; area only for a single series or a true total | bars for 50+ time points |
| a part-of-whole with 2-3 parts | one stacked bar or a labelled fraction | pie/donut with many slices |
| distribution | histogram, strip/dot plot, box plot | a bar of averages that hides spread |
| relation between two measures | scatter plot | two y-axes on one chart |
| a value against a target | bullet bar or a bar with a target tick | gauges |
| a matrix of values | heatmap with a sequential ramp, values on hover | rainbow colour scales |
| one headline value | stat tile, optionally with a sparkline | a chart with one bar |

Rules that hold for every form:

- **One y-scale per chart.** Two measures in different units become two charts stacked or
  side by side, or both indexed to 100 at a common start.
- **Bars start at zero.** Lines may zoom in, but say so on the axis.
- **Sort categorical bars** by value unless the categories have a natural order.
- **More than 8 series** is a different chart: fold the tail into "Other", use small
  multiples, or highlight 1-2 series in colour and draw the rest in muted grey.

## Colour

### The four jobs

- **Categorical (identity):** which series. Slots 1-8 below, assigned in order, never cycled,
  never generated. A series keeps its colour when filters hide its neighbours - map the
  colour to the series key, not to its index in the filtered list.
- **Sequential (magnitude):** one hue, light→dark for "more" on the light surface. In dark
  mode "more" runs toward the *brighter* end, so the dark ramp is its own list.
- **Diverging (polarity):** two hues meeting at a neutral grey midpoint for "zero / on
  target". Never a hue in the middle. Equal step counts per arm.
- **Status:** good / warning / bad keep their own reserved colours (from artifact-design's
  tokens), always paired with an icon or word, and are never reused as "series 4".

A nominal bar chart (teams, endpoints, regions) is **one** series: every bar takes slot 1.
Colouring each bar differently spends the identity channel on nothing.

### Default palette

Surfaces assumed: light `#ffffff`, dark `#15171a`. Swap in your page's surfaces and re-check.

| Slot | Hue | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | blue | `#2563c9` | `#5b9bf0` |
| 2 | green | `#4d7f12` | `#86bd4b` |
| 3 | magenta | `#c0307a` | `#ef6fae` |
| 4 | ochre | `#9a6b00` | `#d8a636` |
| 5 | violet | `#7a4fd6` | `#a98cf6` |
| 6 | teal | `#0f8577` | `#33bba8` |
| 7 | orange | `#c4520c` | `#f08a4b` |
| 8 | slate ("other") | `#5c6878` | `#9aa6b6` |

Sequential (blue, weakest → strongest): light `#dbe7fb #a9c6f3 #6f9fe6 #3a76d2 #1f55a8 #163b75`;
dark `#1c2f4f #22457a #2f62aa #4a83d6 #7aa8ec #b3cdf6`.

Diverging (orange ← grey → blue): light `#b5431a #e0875e #f3c9b6 #d9d9d9 #b6cdf0 #5f93dc #1f55a8`;
dark `#f08a4b #b8603a #6b3f2c #36383c #2f4a73 #3f74c0 #7aa8ec`.

What `node skills/artifact-dataviz/check-contrast.mjs` reports for these values:

- Every categorical slot reaches at least 3:1 against its surface (WCAG 1.4.11 for
  graphical objects): light 4.52-5.67, dark 6.32-8.06.
- Neighbouring slots stay apart (OKLab distance ×100): normal vision ≥ 19.9 light / 17.2 dark;
  simulated protanopia ≥ 12.7 / 12.5; deuteranopia ≥ 10.4 / 12.5.
- **Non-neighbouring** pairs can collide under colour-vision deficiency (worst: slots 3 and 6,
  ΔE 1.2 light / 0.4 dark under deuteranopia; slots 1 and 5 under protanopia). That is why
  the order is fixed and why charts with 3+ series must also carry legends or direct labels.
- Sequential ramps are monotone in lightness; diverging arms step away from the midpoint.
- The lightest sequential steps are deliberately low-contrast fills (heatmap "little"); they
  are not for lines or text.

### Picking or adjusting a colour

1. Start from the slot table; to rebrand, change hue but keep each slot's lightness
   (OKLCH L ≈ 0.50-0.60 on light, 0.68-0.76 on dark) and keep chroma clearly non-grey.
2. Keep the slot order - it is what keeps neighbours distinguishable for colour-blind readers.
3. Run the checker for each mode against the page's real surface:
   `node skills/artifact-dataviz/check-contrast.mjs --bg "#fafaf7" --colors "#...,#..."`
   (add `--text` when the colour will be used for text, which needs 4.5:1). Fix every FAIL;
   do not argue with it.
4. Text never wears a series colour. Values, axis labels and legend text use the text tokens;
   a coloured swatch or line end next to them carries the identity.

### Wiring it as tokens

```css
:root {
  --surface: #ffffff; --ink: #1d1f23; --ink-2: #555a63; --grid: #e6e7ea;
  --s1: #2563c9; --s2: #4d7f12; --s3: #c0307a; --s4: #9a6b00;
  --s5: #7a4fd6; --s6: #0f8577; --s7: #c4520c; --s8: #5c6878;
}
@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) {
  :root {
    --surface: #15171a; --ink: #e9eaec; --ink-2: #a7acb5; --grid: #2a2d33;
    --s1: #5b9bf0; --s2: #86bd4b; --s3: #ef6fae; --s4: #d8a636;
    --s5: #a98cf6; --s6: #33bba8; --s7: #f08a4b; --s8: #9aa6b6;
  }
}
body { background: var(--surface); color: var(--ink); }
```

SVG drawn into the page uses these by name - `fill="var(--s1)"` in an attribute does not
resolve in all browsers, so set colour through CSS classes or `style`:
`.bar { fill: var(--s1) }`, `.axis text { fill: var(--ink-2) }`, or `el.style.fill = 'var(--s3)'`.
`currentColor` is the simplest choice for axes and ink. A theme switch then repaints with no
script. Canvas is the exception: read the tokens with
`getComputedStyle(document.documentElement).getPropertyValue('--s1')` and redraw on
`matchMedia('(prefers-color-scheme: dark)').addEventListener('change', redraw)`.

## Marks, axes, labels

- **Bars:** thin enough that the gap is visible (gap ≈ 30-40 % of the band); 2 px surface-
  coloured gap between stacked segments; rounded corner (≈ 3 px) only on the value end.
- **Lines:** 2 px, round joins; point markers ≥ 8 px only where points matter (sparse data,
  the highlighted point). Surface-coloured 2 px outline on markers that overlap lines.
- **Grid and axes are recessive:** light horizontal gridlines in `--grid`, no chart border,
  no vertical grid for bar charts, no tick marks unless needed. Axis text 11-12 px in `--ink-2`.
- **Ticks** come from a "nice" step (1, 2, 2.5 or 5 × 10ⁿ) aiming at 4-6 ticks. The axis ends
  at the first nice tick at or above the data maximum; no labels past it. Format with
  `Intl.NumberFormat` (`notation: 'compact'` for 12K / 3.4M) and put units in the axis title
  or the first label, not on every tick.
- **Direct labels beat legends** when there are ≤ 4 series: label each line at its right end.
  With 2+ series a legend is still present (a row of swatch + name above the plot); with one
  series no legend - the title names it.
- **Label selectively:** the latest value, the maximum, the one bar the text talks about.
  A number on every bar is a table pretending to be a chart.
- **Titles state the finding** ("p95 latency halved after the cache") and a subtitle states
  units and period.

## Hover, tooltips, interaction

Every plotted SVG chart gets a hover layer by default:

- bars, dots, cells: a tooltip for the mark under the pointer;
- lines and areas: a vertical crosshair snapping to the nearest x, listing every series at it.

Mechanics under our CSP: one absolutely positioned `<div class="tip" role="status">` in the
chart container, filled with `textContent`, moved on `pointermove`, hidden on `pointerleave`.
Use pointer events so touch works; hit targets larger than the mark (transparent full-height
rects per band). Make marks focusable (`tabindex="0"`) and show the same tooltip on `focus`
so keyboard readers get the values. Filters (series toggles, date range) go in one row above
the chart as real `<button>`s or `<select>`s wired with `addEventListener`.

## Example: bar chart from data, with scale and ticks

```html
<figure class="chart" id="latency">
  <figcaption><strong>Checkout is the slowest endpoint</strong><br>
    <span class="sub">p95 latency, ms, last 7 days</span></figcaption>
  <div class="plot"></div>
  <div class="tip" role="status" hidden></div>
</figure>
<style>
  .chart { position: relative; margin: 0; max-width: 720px; }
  .chart svg { display: block; width: 100%; height: auto; overflow: visible; }
  .chart .sub { color: var(--ink-2); font-size: 13px; }
  .chart .axis text { fill: var(--ink-2); font-size: 12px; }
  .chart .grid { stroke: var(--grid); }
  .chart .bar { fill: var(--s1); pointer-events: none; }
  .chart .hit:hover + .bar, .chart .hit:focus-visible + .bar { fill-opacity: .8; }
  .chart .hit { fill: transparent; outline: none; }
  .tip { position: absolute; pointer-events: none; background: var(--surface); color: var(--ink);
         border: 1px solid var(--grid); border-radius: 6px; padding: 4px 8px; font-size: 13px; }
</style>
<script>
const data = [
  { name: 'checkout', v: 812 }, { name: 'search', v: 431 },
  { name: 'cart', v: 268 }, { name: 'login', v: 145 }, { name: 'profile', v: 96 },
];
const NS = 'http://www.w3.org/2000/svg';
const el = (tag, attrs, parent) => {
  const n = document.createElementNS(NS, tag);
  for (const k in attrs) n.setAttribute(k, attrs[k]);
  return parent ? parent.appendChild(n) : n;
};
function niceTicks(max, target = 5) {
  const raw = max / target, mag = 10 ** Math.floor(Math.log10(raw));
  const step = [1, 2, 2.5, 5, 10].map(m => m * mag).find(s => s >= raw);
  const top = Math.ceil(max / step) * step;
  return Array.from({ length: Math.round(top / step) + 1 }, (_, i) => +(i * step).toPrecision(12));
}
const W = 640, rowH = 34, left = 84, right = 16, top = 8, bottom = 26;
const H = top + data.length * rowH + bottom;
const ticks = niceTicks(Math.max(...data.map(d => d.v)));
const x = v => left + (v / ticks.at(-1)) * (W - left - right);
const fmt = new Intl.NumberFormat('en', { notation: 'compact' });

const box = document.querySelector('#latency');
const svg = el('svg', { viewBox: `0 0 ${W} ${H}`, role: 'img',
  'aria-label': data.map(d => `${d.name} ${d.v} ms`).join(', ') }, box.querySelector('.plot'));
const axis = el('g', { class: 'axis' }, svg);
for (const t of ticks) {
  el('line', { class: 'grid', x1: x(t), x2: x(t), y1: top, y2: H - bottom }, axis);
  el('text', { x: x(t), y: H - 8, 'text-anchor': 'middle' }, axis).textContent = fmt.format(t);
}
const tip = box.querySelector('.tip');
data.forEach((d, i) => {
  const y = top + i * rowH, h = rowH * 0.62;
  el('text', { x: left - 8, y: y + rowH / 2 + 4, 'text-anchor': 'end' }, axis)
    .textContent = d.name;
  const hit = el('rect', { class: 'hit', x: left, y, width: W - left - right, height: rowH,
                           tabindex: 0, 'aria-label': `${d.name}: ${d.v} ms` }, svg);
  const bar = el('rect', { class: 'bar', x: left, y: y + (rowH - h) / 2,
                           width: x(d.v) - left, height: h, rx: 3 }, svg);
  const show = (e) => {
    tip.textContent = `${d.name}: ${d.v} ms`; tip.hidden = false;
    const b = box.getBoundingClientRect(), r = bar.getBoundingClientRect();
    const px = e && e.clientX ? e.clientX : r.right;          // pointer, or bar end on focus
    tip.style.left = Math.max(0, Math.min(px - b.left + 8, b.width - 160)) + 'px';
    tip.style.top = (r.top - b.top - 32) + 'px';
  };
  hit.addEventListener('pointermove', show);
  hit.addEventListener('focus', () => show());
  hit.addEventListener('pointerleave', () => { tip.hidden = true; });
  hit.addEventListener('blur', () => { tip.hidden = true; });
});
</script>
```

Note what it avoids: no library, no inline handlers, colours only through tokens, the scale
is one function, tick labels stop at the axis maximum, and the `viewBox` lets the whole
chart shrink to a phone. For a horizontal-label chart that would be unreadable when shrunk,
keep a minimum width and wrap it in a scroll container (below).

## Example: stat tiles with a sparkline

```html
<div class="tiles">
  <div class="tile">
    <div class="label">Weekly publishes</div>
    <div class="value">1,284 <span class="delta up">▲ 12%</span></div>
    <svg class="spark" viewBox="0 0 100 24" preserveAspectRatio="none" role="img"
         aria-label="Weekly publishes over 8 weeks, rising from 870 to 1,284">
      <polyline points="0,20 14,18 28,19 43,14 57,12 71,13 86,7 100,3" />
      <circle cx="100" cy="3" r="2.5" />
    </svg>
  </div>
  <div class="tile"><div class="label">Median page size</div><div class="value">212 KB</div></div>
  <div class="tile"><div class="label">Open comments</div><div class="value">7</div></div>
</div>
<style>
  .tiles { display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(160px, 1fr)); gap: 12px; }
  .tile { border: 1px solid var(--grid); border-radius: 10px; padding: 12px 14px; }
  .tile .label { color: var(--ink-2); font-size: 13px; }
  .tile .value { font-size: 28px; font-weight: 600; font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums; }
  .delta { font-size: 13px; font-weight: 500; color: var(--ink-2); }
  .spark { display: block; width: 100%; height: 28px; margin-top: 6px; color: var(--s1); }
  .spark polyline { fill: none; stroke: currentColor; stroke-width: 2; vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke; }
  .spark circle { fill: currentColor; }
</style>
```

Compute sparkline points from data the same way as the bars: `x = i / (n - 1) * 100`,
`y = 22 - (v - min) / (max - min) * 20`. A sparkline has no axes, so its `aria-label` must
carry the start, end and direction. With `preserveAspectRatio="none"` the dot stretches into
an ellipse at wide sizes; keep it or drop it, but keep `vector-effect` on the line. The delta
arrow plus text carries the direction - never colour alone.

## Phone width and wide content

- Charts: `viewBox` + `width: 100%; height: auto`; design the viewBox at ~640 px wide so text
  stays ≥ 10 px when scaled to a 360 px screen. If it would not, draw a second, narrower
  layout (fewer ticks, horizontal bars instead of columns) chosen by a `matchMedia('(max-width:
  520px)')` listener, or let the chart scroll.
- Wide tables, heatmaps and long time axes: wrap in
  `<div style="overflow-x:auto">` with the inner element at a fixed `min-width`, so the page
  itself never scrolls sideways.
- The viewer's top bar is outside the iframe; do not reserve space for it.

## Vendoring a chart library (only if hand-drawn SVG truly won't do)

It is possible: put the library's `.js` file in the published directory and reference it
with `<script src="vendor/lib.min.js">`; the viewer inlines it. Conditions:

- the file must compress under ~1.99 MB and should be far smaller;
- it must be a classic script (UMD/IIFE global). ES-module builds that `import` relative
  files are not resolved by the viewer;
- it must not use `eval` / `new Function` / WebAssembly - the CSP blocks them. Libraries
  that compile templates, expressions or specs at runtime (some Vega/Vega-Lite paths,
  template-based renderers, older Plotly bundles, Mermaid's heavier diagram types, anything
  WASM-backed) tend to break; small SVG/canvas renderers such as uPlot or a D3 subset
  (scale, shape, axis) usually work;
- it must not fetch fonts, tiles or data from the network, and its export/download buttons
  will not work - hide them.

Test the vendored page through the real viewer before sharing; a library that "works
locally" from `file://` has not met the CSP.

## Accessibility checklist

- The `<svg>` has `role="img"` and an `aria-label` stating the finding, or the figure has a
  caption that does; data-dense charts also get a `<details><summary>Data</summary><table>`.
- Identity never relies on colour alone: legends, direct labels, or marker shape / dash.
- Text ≥ 4.5:1 against the surface (axis text in `--ink-2` included); marks ≥ 3:1.
- Focusable marks show their tooltip on focus; tooltips use `role="status"`.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: no animated bar growth when it is set.
- Under `forced-colors: active`, use `stroke: CanvasText` / `fill: CanvasText` fallbacks for
  marks that would otherwise vanish.

## Before publishing

- [ ] Form matches the job; one y-scale; bars from zero; ≤ 8 series.
- [ ] Palette from this file or re-validated with `check-contrast.mjs` for both surfaces.
- [ ] Light and dark both look right (toggle the OS / devtools emulation and reload).
- [ ] 360 px wide: nothing overflows; text still readable; wide parts scroll inside a box.
- [ ] No `https://` script/style/font URLs (the publish CLI warns), no `on*=` attributes,
      no download buttons, no `localStorage` (use `window.artifact.data` if state must persist).
- [ ] Hover and focus show values; a table or caption gives the numbers to non-visual readers.
